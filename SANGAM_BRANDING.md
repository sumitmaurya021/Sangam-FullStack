# 🌟 Sangam - Social Media Platform

## 📖 About the Name

**Sangam** (संगम) is a beautiful Sanskrit/Hindi word meaning:
- **Confluence** - Where rivers meet
- **Meeting Point** - Where people come together
- **Union** - Connection of minds and hearts
- **Gathering** - Community and togetherness

Perfect for a social media platform that brings people together! 🇮🇳

## 💡 Brand Story

Sangam was born from the idea that social media should be more than just connections - it should be a true meeting point where people come together, share experiences, and build meaningful relationships.

Just like the sacred Sangam where rivers meet, our platform is where people from different backgrounds, cultures, and experiences converge to create something beautiful together.

Built with Indian values of community and togetherness, Sangam is designed for the modern world while staying true to the timeless principle that we are stronger together.
